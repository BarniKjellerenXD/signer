Register this iPhone

This reads your iPhone's device ID (UDID) through a temporary Apple profile and adds it to the signing profile. Nothing stays installed.

What happens next

  1. Safari asks to download a configuration profile: tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded at the top, then Install and enter your passcode. It's shown as "Unverified", which is normal.
  3. Safari reopens with your UDID. The server registers it with Apple and re-signs the apps, which takes about a minute.

Must be done in Safari with Tailscale on. If nothing happens after Install, open this page again and retry.
